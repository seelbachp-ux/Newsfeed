**Anthropic's stock opens 34% up, then gives back half the gain** — ANT opened on Nasdaq Thursday at $111.50 against the $83 IPO price and briefly implied a roughly $2 trillion valuation. It closed at $97.17, up 17%, as major US indexes fell. Including a $26B private placement, the listing raised $107.3B in total.
*Why it matters:* the market now prices a frontier lab every day, and PitchBook expects lockup expiries around the first earnings reports in 2027 to test that price.
Source: [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/markets/2026/11/20/anthropic-share-price-today-ant-listing/)

**Apple job listings describe an autonomous, multi-agent assistant** — New listings, first reported by Bloomberg, describe agents that "plan and carry out multi-step tasks across apps, browsers, command lines, code, and APIs" and coordinate sub-agents. Mark Gurman says some of this could ship as soon as next year, but the wording suggests the work is early.
*Why it matters:* if Siri starts driving apps on its own, App Intents and the way apps expose actions become more important to indie developers than any chatbot integration.
Source: [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/report-apple-job-listings-detail-plans-for-autonomous-ai-agents/)

**DeepSeek closes a ~$15.8B second round and prepares a STAR Market IPO** — Demand pushed the round to about 105.5B yuan, roughly double its target, for about $23.5B raised in total this year. Tencent and CATL are the largest outside backers. DeepSeek has hired CITIC Securities for a Shanghai listing.
*Why it matters:* China's leading open-weight lab now has funding on a US-lab scale, which keeps pressure on API prices across the market.
Source: [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/94d7773a-2959-4c23-962e-19a603560ac2)

**Fireworks AI says open models trail the frontier by only three to six months** — Fireworks co-founder Chenyu Zhao said most companies can skip frontier models. He suggests switching to tuned open models at roughly 10K–100K users or $10K–100K a month in token spend. TechCrunch also covers Fireworks' two security incidents this fall, which exposed some Notion chat data and data on about 150K Uber drivers.
*Why it matters:* the cost argument for open models is getting stronger, but the vendor's security record matters as much as the price.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropic-rival-says-companies-can-mostly-skip-the-pricey-models/)

**Superhuman (formerly Grammarly) adds its Go assistant to Docs and Mail, plus 20 partner agents** — Go now drafts and triages email proactively, runs background tasks triggered by events, and takes notes in meetings. Partners including DeepL, Databricks and Amplitude plug in through the Go SDK. The company claims more than 1M active users.
*Why it matters:* another large app is turning itself into a platform where third-party agents run inside it, an approach worth watching for anyone who builds agents.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/superhuman-pushes-its-go-assistant-everywhere/)

**House report: the US approved 879,000+ AI chip sales to China** — Figures released by the House Foreign Affairs Committee show Commerce approved about one in four of the roughly 3.4M Nvidia and AMD requests filed between December and May. Some approvals went to firms the Pentagon lists as Chinese military companies, and two buyers are suspected smuggling fronts.
*Why it matters:* this is a new front in the export-control fight after Nvidia's halt, and it raises the odds of tighter rules in the next Congress.
Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/nvidia-amd-china-chip-sales-congress.html)
