**Anthropic prices the largest IPO on record** — Anthropic sold about 980 million shares at $83, above its $70–78 range, raising roughly $81.3 billion; shares begin trading on Nasdaq today under the ticker ANT.
*Why it matters:* It tops SpaceX's record raise and gives the public market its first direct read on what a frontier lab is worth.
Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)

**Google ships Gemini 4 Neon** — A new mid-tier Gemini 4 model aimed at coding and complex analysis, with a 1M-token context at $3/$15 per million input/output tokens. It is rolling out in the Gemini API, AI Studio, Antigravity and paid Gemini plans, where it replaces Argon for AI Pro users.
*Why it matters:* Independent benchmarks put it just ahead of Google's own top-tier Carbon, so near-flagship coding performance now comes at a lower price.
Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/)

**Nvidia stops shipping AI chips to China** — Interim CEO Colette Kress said no more licensed Hopper shipments are planned, even though Washington had cleared some sales. Nvidia's Q4 guidance of about $130B assumes zero China data-center revenue. Shares closed down about 2.9% despite a record $115.8B quarter.
*Why it matters:* The world's main AI-chip supplier is giving up on China entirely, which will likely speed up China's push to build its own chips.
Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/nvidia-chips-china-military-ai.html)

**Banks say AI-agent users are on their own** — Citi updated its terms so that customers are liable for activity carried out by third-party AI tools with account access, even if those tools are compromised. Other banks are taking a similar line.
*Why it matters:* This is the first clear sign of who bears the risk when agents act on your behalf. Anyone building agents that touch money will have to account for it.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/more-banks-says-customers-using-ai-agents-are-on-their-own-even-if-they-get-hacked/)

**Palo Alto Networks buys Applied Compute for $4.5B** — Alongside a strong earnings beat (revenue up 39%), the cybersecurity firm announced an all-stock deal for the AI startup, expected to close in its fiscal Q3.
*Why it matters:* Security vendors are buying AI teams outright to deal with AI-driven threats, and the startup was valued at a price few AI companies have reached.
Source: [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/11/63414200/palo-alto-posts-q1-double-beat-announces-applied-compute-acquisition)

**Early testers sour on GPT-6.2 Astra** — Developers report that OpenAI's new flagship asks for permission too often and struggles with long autonomous workflows. OpenAI says the models changed substantially in the final days before launch.
*Why it matters:* For agent work, how reliably a model finishes delegated tasks matters more than benchmark scores, and here the first reports are mixed.
Source: [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/anthropic-could-be-the-real-winner-in-openais-latest-model-release-2000841822)

**SenseTime open-sources a 9B "looped" model** — SenseNova Loop-9B reuses its layers to compute more deeply without adding parameters. It takes images and text with a context of up to 512K tokens.
*Why it matters:* Small open models that get more out of the same parameters are what make capable on-device AI realistic.
Source: [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/11/18/sensetime-releases-open-weight-loop-9b-model-that-reuses-layers-for-deeper-computation/)
